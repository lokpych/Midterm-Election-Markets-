Methodology & limitations

Historical facts, reconstructed industry proxies, market benchmarks, and forecasts are labeled separately. No causal inference is implied solely by co-movement.

Historical series. 1971–1995 uses Kenneth French’s five-industry, value-weighted research portfolios. 1999–2023 uses sector ETF proxies, not an officially harmonized GICS backtest. Real Estate uses VNQ in 2007–2015. Missing histories are shown as unavailable. Equity, Treasury and Baa returns are not guaranteed to use identical constituent or reinvestment conventions.

Forecasts. 2027 bull/base/bear figures are illustrative conditional total-return assumptions derived for the accompanying research, not J.P. Morgan forecasts or historical observations. The 2026 congressional outcome is a preliminary post-election context as of the report date; confirm final certified results separately.
